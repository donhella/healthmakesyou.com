Why menopause joint pain flares up (and how to calm it)

17 Nov 2026 · 11:29

About this video

Menopause joint pain is one of the most common symptoms of this stage of life, yet one of the least talked about. Learn why it flares up and how to calm it.

Stiff fingers in the morning, painful heels on your first steps out of bed, aching hips at night or a frozen shoulder can all be linked to falling estrogen. This video explains how hormone changes affect your cartilage, tendons, muscles and sleep, why the pain comes and goes, and the warning signs that point to something else, like rheumatoid arthritis or polymyalgia rheumatica, so you know when to get checked.

We also look at what the research says about hormone therapy and the everyday steps with the strongest evidence: strength training, daily movement, enough protein, a Mediterranean-style diet, checking your vitamin D and protecting your sleep. This video is for women going through perimenopause and menopause, and for the people who love them. Always talk to your doctor before making changes to your treatment.
