I'm 32. Here's what I wish I knew in my 20s

6 Aug 2026 · 13:04

About this video

At 32, I can look back at my 20s and clearly see the habits, choices, and health decisions I wish I had taken more seriously. Some things feel small when you’re younger, but they can shape how you feel years later.

In this video, I’m sharing the lessons I wish I understood earlier about sleep, movement, food, screen time, stress, and taking care of your body before problems start showing up.

If you’re in your 20s, this is the kind of advice that can save you from learning everything the hard way. And if you’re already in your 30s or beyond, there’s still plenty you can start changing now.
