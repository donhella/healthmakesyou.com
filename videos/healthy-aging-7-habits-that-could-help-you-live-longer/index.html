Healthy aging: 7 habits that could help you live longer than 70

7 Sep 2026 · 11:40

About this video

Healthy aging starts with the everyday choices you make. If you want to live longer than 70 and enjoy better health as you grow older, there are simple habits you can start building today.

In this video, I share 7 healthy aging habits that could help you live longer, stay physically active, protect your health, and improve your quality of life. We'll explore practical lifestyle changes that support longevity, from nutrition and exercise to sleep and other daily habits that can make a difference over time.

Living longer isn't just about adding more years to your life. It's about staying healthy enough to enjoy those years.
